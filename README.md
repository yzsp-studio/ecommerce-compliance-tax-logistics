## 🇨🇳 中文

把跨境卖家的合规准入、税务与知识产权、海关与物流清关合成一条以商品、国家、平台和履约路线为中心的工作流。它先做准入与限制预筛（禁限售、认证、标签、宣称、危险品、侵权与品牌授权），再处理 VAT/GST/Sales Tax、OSS/IOSS、Nexus 与商标风险，最后落到 HS 编码、关税、落地成本、商业发票、COO、DDP/DDU、FBA/FBM、海外仓与 3PL。凡涉及平台规则、税率、注册门槛、强制认证、HS/关税或申报状态的结论，都必须绑定官方或第一手来源与核验日期，查不到就标为未验证，不让一个 SKU 的文件状态套用到另一个 SKU。上架、发运、入仓或付费推广前需依次通过强制闸门，未通过则输出 HOLD、NO-GO 或 CONDITIONAL TEST，并列明关闭证据与责任人。表格、发票、装箱单等多文件任务会先做跨文件对账、差异表与合计算校验，再产 Word/Excel/CSV 并自检公式错误。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入商品/国家/平台/履约路线 → 输出准入、税务与清关路径判断
- 准入预筛：禁限售、认证、标签、宣称、危险品、侵权与品牌授权
- 税务与 IP：VAT/GST/Sales Tax、OSS/IOSS、Nexus、商标与灰市/假货风险
- 关务与物流：HS 编码、关税、落地成本、商业发票、COO、DDP/DDU、FBA/FBM、海外仓、3PL
- 决策级结论绑定官方/第一手来源与核验日期，查不到即标未验证
- 多文件任务先对账与合计算校验、交付物自检公式错误；强制闸门未过输出 HOLD / NO-GO / CONDITIONAL TEST 并列出关闭证据与责任人

## 🇬🇧 English

Merges cross-border compliance and product eligibility, tax and IP risk, and customs and logistics clearance into a single workflow centred on product, country, platform and fulfilment route. It pre-screens admission and restrictions (prohibited or restricted goods, certification, labelling, claims, dangerous goods, infringement and brand authorisation), then handles VAT/GST/Sales Tax, OSS/IOSS, Nexus and trademark risk, and finally HS code, tariff, landed cost, commercial invoice, COO, DDP/DDU, FBA/FBM, overseas warehouse and 3PL. Any decision-critical statement about platform rules, tax rates, registration thresholds, mandatory certification, HS/tariff or filing status must be tied to an official or first-party source with a verification date; what cannot be checked is marked unverified, and one SKU's document status is never applied to another. Mandatory gates must be cleared in order before listing, shipment, warehouse inbound or paid launch; otherwise the answer is HOLD, NO-GO or CONDITIONAL TEST with the exact closing evidence and owner. Spreadsheet, invoice and packing-list work is reconciled across files with control-total validation before Word/Excel/CSV deliverables are produced and self-checked for formula errors.

### ✨ What it does

- Product, country, platform and fulfilment route in → an admission, tax and clearance path out
- Admission pre-screen: prohibited/restricted goods, certification, labelling, claims, dangerous goods, infringement, brand authorisation
- Tax and IP: VAT/GST/Sales Tax, OSS/IOSS, Nexus, trademark plus grey-market and counterfeit risk
- Customs and logistics: HS code, tariff, landed cost, commercial invoice, COO, DDP/DDU, FBA/FBM, overseas warehouse, 3PL
- Binds decision-critical conclusions to an official or first-party source and verification date, flagging what cannot be verified
- Reconciles multi-file inputs with control totals and self-checks deliverables for formula errors; emits HOLD / NO-GO / CONDITIONAL TEST while a mandatory gate is open, with the closing evidence and owner
